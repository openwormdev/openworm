WHAT IT IS

A real model,
not a metaphor

This laboratory uses c302’s C1 equations and its default historical connectivity reader. The sensory currents are engineered; the full selected network propagates them; a fixed readout compares named forward and reverse command neurons.

It does not recreate all of a living worm’s physiology. Synaptic parameters, financial inputs, and readout thresholds carry substantial modelling assumptions. Zero trades are a valid result.

WHAT IS NOT REAL, STATED PLAINLY

The parts that
aren’t the worm

  1. The market senses are designed.Price gradients, flow, liquidity, and impact become bounded currents chosen for this experiment.
  2. The worm does not understand finance.It has no language, goals, wallet, or concept of a token. The readout is an imposed interpretation of command-neuron activity.
  3. The browser replays recordings.Bundled files contain model traces already calculated through c302, jNeuroML, and NEURON. Playback is not a browser simulation.
  4. The executor is paper-only.No order-signing method exists. Fills, fees, slippage, impact, balances, and P&L are fictional and fully auditable.
